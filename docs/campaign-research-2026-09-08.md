# Earth Guardians campaign research

Research date: 2026-09-08.

The official Earth Guardians homepage describes the organization as a youth-led environmental nonprofit focused on climate-justice solutions, with resources and training for local, national, and international action. Its theory of change combines education, micro-grants, network connections, public speaking, training, campaigns, partnerships, and mentorship. The homepage highlights youth leadership, crews, direct community impact, reforestation, and access for marginalized communities.

The official resources page presents campaigns as one part of a broader action system alongside Crews, Project Grants, project guides and toolkits, events, videos, blogs, global alignment, and speakers. This indicates that the application should not position endangered species as the entire campaigns system. It should be presented as one biodiversity-focused campaign or data program within a larger campaign library.

The official Choose Action Now (CAN) campaign page describes CAN as a global day of action in which youth leaders and partners organize grassroots projects, creative solutions, and community resilience. Its materials include a day-of-action toolkit, direct-action inspiration, a platform of demands, and project examples such as tree planting, regenerative agriculture, workshops, rallies, and benefit events. Its stated themes include rights of nature, ecocide accountability, fossil-fuel phase-out, loss and damage, Indigenous rights, climate refugees, food sovereignty, and climate education.

The official sustainable solutions page groups related work around waste reduction, sustainable products and services, civic engagement, policy change, biodiversity loss, water scarcity, deforestation, soil degradation, EarthTracks, environmental audits, and community resources. This is a useful model for a campaign taxonomy organized around action themes rather than only map datasets.

The official youth leadership training page connects campaigns to capacity-building. It describes campaign development, project management, storytelling, public speaking, holistic leadership, conflict management, and mentorship. Training participants can develop local action plans, amplify CAN, and become eligible for Project Grants. The application should therefore connect campaign discovery to three actions: learn, organize with a Crew, and fund a project.
